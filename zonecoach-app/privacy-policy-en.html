Privacy Policy

ZoneCoach ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is handled.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

1.1 Health Data (HealthKit)

The App integrates with Apple’s HealthKit API on your device to access the following data.

Read

  • Heart rate: Used to determine your heart-rate zone in real time during workouts and provide voice coaching.
  • Date of birth, biological sex, and body mass: Used for profile-based calculations such as zone estimates and calorie estimates.

Write (iPhone is the system of record)

  • Workouts: Saves session results such as duration to the Health app.
  • Heart-rate samples: Saves measured heart-rate samples to Health.
  • Active energy (calories): Saves estimated active energy to Health.

The Apple Watch companion reads heart rate from the optical sensor and relays it to iPhone. It does not finalize/save the workout record to Health; the iPhone app performs Health writes.

1.2 Bluetooth Heart Rate Monitors

The App uses Bluetooth to receive heart rate from compatible BLE heart-rate monitors. This data is processed only on your device and is not sent to external servers.

1.3 Apple Watch Connectivity

The App uses Watch Connectivity and HealthKit’s Watch launch APIs to receive heart rate from ZoneCoach on Apple Watch. Health data obtained via Watch is processed only on-device and is not transmitted externally.

1.4 On-Device Workout History

As a premium feature, summaries of completed workouts may be stored locally in the App on your device. This history is not sent to external servers.

1.5 Background Modes

  • audio: Deliver voice alerts while the screen is locked.
  • bluetooth-central: Maintain connections to BLE heart-rate monitors.
  • workout-processing (Watch): Continue heart-rate measurement during a workout.

1.6 Advertising and Tracking (ATT)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements for free users. If you grant permission via iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT), the App may access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to deliver personalized ads.

2. Handling of HealthKit Data (Important)

  • Local processing only: Heart rate and other health data are processed entirely on your device (iPhone and Apple Watch).
  • No sharing with third parties: Health data is never sold, shared, disclosed, or transferred to any third party.
  • No use for advertising: Health data is never used for targeted advertising or user profiling.

3. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Apple’s StoreKit system. Payment details are managed solely by Apple.

4. Other Data

The App may receive anonymous crash reports provided by Apple. These do not contain personally identifiable information.

5. Changing Permissions

  • Health: Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices
  • Bluetooth: Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth
  • Tracking (ads): Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be changed without prior notice as needed. Changes take effect when published on this page.
