Privacy Policy

MuteCam ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect and Use

1.1 Camera and Photo Library Access

The App requires access to the following device permissions to provide silent photo capture functionality:

  • Camera: Used solely for live camera preview and capturing photo frames without shutter sound.
  • Photo Library Additions: Used to save captured photos directly to your device's Photos app.

All captured photos and camera preview streams are processed and stored exclusively on your device. We do not transmit, collect, or store your photos on any external servers.

1.2 Location Data (GPS)

If you explicitly enable location tagging in the App settings, geotagging information (latitude and longitude) will be embedded in the EXIF metadata of photos you take. This data remains strictly stored within the local photo file on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

1.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use device identifiers or cookies to serve ads based on user interactions.

For more information on Google's privacy practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps

1.4 In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

In-app purchases (MuteCam Pro) are securely processed by Apple Inc. via StoreKit. We do not collect or have access to your credit card information or payment details.

2. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside third parties, except as required by law.

3. Managing Device Permissions

You can manage or revoke camera, photo library, or location permissions at any time via your iOS Device Settings:

  • Go to Settings > MuteCam to modify camera and photo library access.
  • Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services to adjust location settings.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
